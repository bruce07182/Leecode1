Math reference

Calculus topics used when studying electric potential.

Derivatives

A derivative measures the rate of change at a point: the slope of a curve.

dV/dx

Physics: Eₓ = −dV/dx. For V(x)=12−3x volts, Eₓ=+3 V/m.

Gradient

The gradient collects partial derivatives of a scalar field into a vector.

E⃗ = −∇V

Physics: The electric field points toward decreasing potential. In one dimension, Eₓ=−dV/dx.

Example

For V(x,y)=3x²+2y volts, ∇V=(6x,2) V/m, so E⃗=(−6x,−2) V/m.
