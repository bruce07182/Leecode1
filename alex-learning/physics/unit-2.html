Unit 2 — Electric Potential

Idea: Electric potential is electric potential energy per unit charge, similar to height on a hill. Voltage is the difference in potential between two points.

Point charge potential:

\\[V=\\frac{kQ}{r}\\]
Potential energy:
\\[U=qV\\]
Change in potential energy:
\\[\\Delta U=q\\Delta V\\]

Move away from a positive charge

Example: A +2 nC source charge gives approximately 18 V at 1 m and 9 V at 2 m. Doubling distance halves potential, but quarters electric field.

Connection: Electric fields point toward decreasing electric potential. For a uniform field along x:

\\[E_x=-\\frac{dV}{dx}\\]
